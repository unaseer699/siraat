# 1. ORIGINAL PRODUCT VISION (Version 0)

**What Siraat is:** Pakistan's first AI-powered Property Intelligence Platform. Not a listings portal — an intelligence layer helping people decide *what* to buy, *why*, and *how* to complete the journey from investment to construction.

**Vision:** Every Pakistani should be able to make confident property decisions using trusted data and artificial intelligence.

**Mission:** Simplify property buying, selling, investing, and construction through intelligent recommendations instead of overwhelming listings.

**The Problem (original):** Buyers face thousands of listings with no guidance, no way to judge fair pricing, limited visibility into legal status, no reliable construction estimates, no centralized material pricing, difficulty comparing societies objectively, poor access for overseas Pakistanis, and no trusted contractor information.

**Original five-product ecosystem:**
1. AI Property Advisor
2. Property Intelligence (scores, trends, comparisons)
3. Construction Intelligence (cost estimation, BOQ, materials, timelines)
4. Trust Layer (legal verification, developer profiles, checklists)
5. Marketplace (contractors, architects, suppliers, banks, legal pros)

**Target customers:** Primary — Overseas Pakistanis, Home Buyers, Home Builders. Secondary — Investors, Builders, Developers, Banks, Contractors, Architects, Agencies.

**Original guiding principles:** Intelligence before inventory. Trust before transactions. Recommendations before listings. Data before opinions. AI explains every recommendation. Transparency builds confidence.

**North Star Metric:** The number of property decisions influenced by Siraat.

**Founder Principle:** *"We are not building Pakistan's biggest property website. We are building Pakistan's smartest property intelligence company."*

**Strategic refinement (mid-project, incorporated before Phase 6):**
- Siraat repositioned as a **Property & Construction Intelligence Platform** — not competing with Zameen/Graana on listings. Category begins *after* a customer finds a property.
- **Trust is the product.** Philosophy: *"Don't trust us. Verify us."*
- **Zoraiz Developers** (founder's construction company) integrated into the data strategy as a first-party operational-data engine (material consumption, supplier pricing, labor productivity, delays, invoices) — feeding a future **Siraat Construction OS (SCOS)**, acknowledged but explicitly deferred (see Phase 5).
- Governance rule established: `is_siraat_affiliated` disclosure flag — affiliation never influences scoring; evidence determines trust, not ownership (hard law, Phase 5).
- **Later product-phase crystallization (Phase 12):** Siraat's philosophy distilled to one sentence — *"People don't buy property. They buy confidence."* Siraat is **the Trust Operating System for Real Estate.**
